TRAINING ARC 0.53.1

Alpha4 November 2026

The studio got rebuilt, room by room. A lot has changed since the last build (0.47.0). Here is the rundown. Your save loads straight in, with all its progress.

The studio

  • The main floor is the network's hall now, with the lobby's look: steel panels, a window band onto the city and light that changes through the day. Walk east out of the lobby through an open arch into the stair hall, then on to the Stage door, the Control Room, the Quick Change counter (Wardrobe & Props) and the back door to the dockside.
  • Real stairs: walk up both flights to the 2nd floor. No more walking through the railings.
  • The Control Room is one room: Master Ponzu by his armchair, the Remote under the monitor wall, the red phone, the Crew Board and, at the far end, the Powerhouse with the drills and the gravity chamber. When the show is live, ON AIR lights up and the house lights go down.
  • The Stage is a TV studio seen from behind the scenes: the wings, the curtain, the audience in rows. Coming home from an episode puts you on the Stage, live, and the audience applauds. You can collect and tune in to the next episode right there.
  • The backlot is redrawn: wider, with fireworks after dark and the crew's trailers lit when someone's resting inside.
  • The dockside is a long pier with the news chopper and a bigger boat. Expeditions start at the boat.
  • The 2nd floor is the network's office wing, with windows across the main floor and a corridor of doors.
  • The roof is rebuilt: the Bean Garden grows into a greenhouse, and the lookout at the east end opens up the whole bay, the bridge and the city. The moonlight shimmers on the water at night.
  • The Trophy Room (2nd floor) is the network's awards hall: 19 trophies to earn. Each one waits in its case until you walk up and unveil it. The Wall of Fame shows your achievements and the Relic Shelf your relics.
  • The crew don't bunch up any more: everyone stands by their own station.

Getting started

  • The first hours teach one thing at a time: your first fight, one drill, your first upgrade, then splitting your time between two drills.
  • The call sheet suggests zones you can actually take.
  • Your first recruit opens the Training Yard at zone 3, and new recruits catch up quickly at first.

Fights and loot

  • Warnings show before attack hints and boss banners, so the important thing reads first.
  • First-clear rewards say what you got.
  • Solari's uppercut, tornado kick and Shockwave animate more clearly.
  • Higher airings drop more and better gear, and the end game after the Season Finale keeps scaling up.
  • The Season Finale tells you what you keep and what restarts before you confirm.

Menus

  • A new title screen: an anime title card with the cast, PRESS START, and your save slots as episode cards.
  • One welcome-back card: WHILE YOU WERE AWAY adds up what your time away paid. The PREVIOUSLY ON screen is gone.
  • Save & quit asks where to: the desktop, the main menu, or back to the game.
  • A tidier pause menu: pages you can reach in the studio leave it once their place is open.

Cut

  • The Post Office: mail, quests and the QP shop. Old saves keep every perk they bought, and leftovers are paid out in Rin.
  • The Koi Pond, and the minigames Pressure Valve, Sorting Rush, Haggle and Live Read. Saves carry over.

Plus a lot of fixes.
