TRAINING ARC 0.56.0

Alpha7 November 2026

Six fighters, six power-ups. A lot has changed since the last build (0.53.1). Here is the rundown. Your save loads straight in, with all its progress.

Fighters

  • Every power-up is a scene from that fighter's show: the Solari's sunrise, the Mossborn's roots and leaves, the Forecaster's storm, the Funsuit's costume change with confetti, the Gearborn overclocking with steam and sparks, and the Moonveil's moonlight. Charges, ki blasts and dashes sound different per fighter too.
  • The Solari moves like a real fighter now (the base form; the transformations get the same treatment next): a new walk, punches, kicks, uppercut, flurry, charge and reveal, with real shoes, hands and elbows and a body that turns into its blows.
  • Lens Flare is a showpiece move: shades on, a big flash that dazzles every foe on screen, and they back off while it plays.
  • Gear fits the new body. Long robes cover the back leg and open at the front for kicks; hats sit on the head and hide the hair under them.
  • Elites keep their real colours and get a gold glow behind them, instead of being washed in gold.
  • A beaten boss plays its whole K.O. before it fades.

Power and progress

  • PL is a whole number from the start. A new fighter starts at PL 100, every K.O. adds whole points, and very big numbers read like "80B". Bonuses read as percentages ("+10% PL gain"), and gear shows how much better or worse it is than what you're wearing. Your power didn't change, only how it's shown.
  • Recommended PL for every zone, on the Remote and on the call sheet.
  • The Season Finale and the Reboot are gone. Your run keeps going, forever. Unspent Ratings and Reels, and any upgrade that was cut, turn into Rin once, and a save that ever ended a season keeps a "Season Pass" title. Boss Rush now rewards crit chance.
  • Every permanent upgrade is on the Producer's Phone, with the same prices and effects.
  • The end game takes longer to climb: the top gear tier is a real farming goal now, not an afternoon.

The studio and menus

  • TRAINING ARC: CHANNEL FIGHTERS. The game has a subtitle, a new logo and a new icon (an old wood-cabinet TV wearing the hero's headband).
  • A new title screen: the studio floor at night, with lit beams, dust in the light, moving cameras, a monitor and a real ON AIR light. Your save slots are studio monitors.
  • The call sheet is a slate in the corner. It takes much less of the screen, and every line is a plain sentence. Hold View for the run of show.
  • One set of Settings for the whole game, whichever save you load.
  • Photo mode stays sharp when you zoom, and you can zoom in further.
  • The main floor's door upstairs is redrawn, its office windows show the real office, and upstairs the windows sit on a half wall.
  • Clean-up: the last of the old idle leftovers are gone (the TODAY board, the GOALS tab, the old season card). The Studio Head's old office is the show's poster wall now.

Plus a lot of fixes.

Still in the works: the animations and artwork are being actively reworked and will keep getting updates over the coming weeks. There's still a lot of fine tuning to do, plus replacements and new content, so a few things may look in-between for a while.
